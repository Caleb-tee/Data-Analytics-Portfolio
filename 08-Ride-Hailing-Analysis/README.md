#  Ride-Hailing Travel Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analysed ride-hailing journeys to evaluate travel behaviour, mileage patterns, journey purposes, and operational efficiency.

---

##  Business Problem

Identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary travel and improve transport efficiency.

---

##  Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Dashboard

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Standardised dates.
- Cleaned ride categories.
- Validated mileage and duration.

---

##  Analysis

Business and personal rides were analysed by mileage, purpose, time of day, and trip anomalies.

##  Key Insights

- Business trips accounted for most travel.
- Meetings represented the most common purpose.
- Afternoon recorded the highest ride activity.
- Long-distance trips were predominantly business-related.

---

##  Recommendations

- Optimise travel planning.
- Reduce unnecessary mileage.
- Monitor unusually long journeys.
