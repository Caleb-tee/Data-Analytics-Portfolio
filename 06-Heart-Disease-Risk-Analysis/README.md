#  Heart Disease Risk Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project explored the relationship between lifestyle factors, clinical indicators, and heart disease risk.

---

## Business Problem

Identify risk factors associated with cardiovascular disease to support preventive healthcare strategies.

---

##  Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Dashboard

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Standardised categories.
- Addressed missing values.
- Validated clinical variables.

---

##  Analysis

Heart disease prevalence was analysed across age, BMI, cholesterol, diabetes, smoking, exercise, stress, and family history.

##  Key Insights

- Older patients recorded higher heart disease prevalence.
- Smoking, diabetes, obesity, and high cholesterol increased risk.
- Regular exercise was associated with lower disease prevalence.

---

##  Recommendations

- Promote preventive screening.
- Encourage healthier lifestyles.
- Target high-risk populations.
