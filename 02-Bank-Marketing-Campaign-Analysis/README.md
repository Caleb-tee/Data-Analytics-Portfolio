#  Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analysed a bank marketing campaign dataset to identify the customer, financial, and campaign characteristics that influenced term deposit subscriptions. The objective was to understand customer behaviour, evaluate campaign effectiveness, and identify segments with the highest likelihood of subscribing.

---

##  Business Problem

The bank wanted to determine:
- Which customers were most likely to subscribe.
- How demographic and financial characteristics influenced subscription.
- Which campaign channels and months achieved the best results.
- Opportunities to improve future marketing campaigns.

---

##  Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Dashboard
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualisation

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Standardised column names and formats.
- Created age and balance categories.
- Removed inconsistencies.
- Validated categorical variables.
- Created calculated fields for customer segmentation.

---

##  Analysis

Customer demographics, financial characteristics, campaign interactions, contact methods, and subscription outcomes were analysed using PivotTables and dashboards. Comparisons were made across age groups, occupations, education levels, marital status, account balance, contact methods, previous campaign outcomes, and campaign months.

##  Key Insights

- Young and senior customers recorded stronger subscription rates.
- Customers with tertiary education responded more positively.
- Cellular contact generated the highest subscriptions.
- Campaign performance varied significantly by month.
- Customers with medium and high balances showed stronger responses.

---

##  Recommendations

- Target high-converting customer segments.
- Prioritise cellular communication.
- Schedule campaigns during high-performing months.
- Personalise offers using customer segmentation.
