#  Fish Distribution Retail Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project evaluated sales performance, supplier efficiency, customer purchasing behaviour, and profitability across a Nigerian fish distribution business.

---

##  Business Problem

The business wanted to improve profitability, inventory planning, supplier performance, and customer satisfaction.

---

##  Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Dashboard

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Standardised dates.
- Validated numeric fields.
- Calculated transaction profit.
- Cleaned supplier and customer records.

---

##  Analysis

Sales, profits, suppliers, fish types, customer locations, discounts, and purchasing behaviour were analysed to evaluate business performance.

##  Key Insights

- Lagos generated the highest sales.
- Supplier performance varied considerably.
- Moderate discounts increased sales.
- Customer demand differed by location.

---

##  Recommendations

- Optimise inventory.
- Strengthen supplier partnerships.
- Expand high-performing locations.
- Review discount strategies.
